## Overview

This chapter explores how Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) enhance digital forensics by automating evidence analysis, detecting anomalies, identifying attack patterns, and classifying malware.

---

## Key Topics

- Introduction to AI/ML concepts relevant to forensics
- Automating evidence triage and analysis
- Anomaly and pattern detection in large datasets
- AI-assisted malware classification
- Natural language processing for evidence review (e.g. emails, chats)
- Limitations, bias, and legal admissibility of AI-derived evidence
