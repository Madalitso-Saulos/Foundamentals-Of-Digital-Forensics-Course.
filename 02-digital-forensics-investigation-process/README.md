## Overview

This chapter covers the structured methodology forensic investigators follow when handling a case, from the moment an incident is identified through to reporting findings in court or to an organization.

---

## Key Topics

- Identification of a digital forensic incident
- Preservation of digital evidence and the crime scene
- Collection of evidence (imaging, acquisition methods)
- Examination and analysis of collected evidence
- Documentation throughout the investigation
- Presentation and reporting of findings
- Chain of custody procedures
