## Overview

Email forensics examines email communication to identify evidence related to cyber incidents. This chapter looks at how investigators analyze email messages, headers, attachments, and sender/receiver information.

---

## Key Topics

- Email architecture and protocols (SMTP, IMAP, POP3)
- Analyzing email headers and metadata
- Investigating email spoofing and phishing
- Recovering deleted or hidden emails
- Email forensic tools and techniques
- Case study: tracing a phishing campaign
